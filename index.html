One example

One real result from my day job, in one story.

I automated our daily platform health checks.

40minutes

saved every working day, about 170 hours a year

The problem

Every working day began with someone logging in to two platforms and clicking through health checks by hand.

What I did

I had never used an API. I learned the Palo Alto and Ivanti REST APIs from their documentation, taught myself Ansible, and built checks that email the whole team an HTML report on a schedule. Then I wrote the runbook.

The result

Anyone on the team can see platform health from anywhere without logging in, and anyone can maintain the checks.
